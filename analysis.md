# Analysis

## Layer 2, Head 4

This attention head focuses on determiners (like "the") and the nouns that come after them. In the examples below, the word "the" gives most of its attention to the noun it is describing, like "table" or "ground". This suggests the model understands that determiners usually go with specific nouns.

Example Sentences:

- The cat jumped on the [MASK].
- He picked up the [MASK] from the shelf.

## Layer 5, Head 7

This attention head looks like it connects subjects (like "she" or "he") to the verbs that follow. In both examples, the subject is paying most attention to its verb, showing that the model has learned how subjects and verbs go together in a sentence.

Example Sentences:

- She [MASK] the window carefully.
- He [MASK] to the store yesterday.
